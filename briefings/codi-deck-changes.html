Consistency

WhereChange
All 7 em dashes (—) changed to en dashes (–), on slides and in the notes. You type en dashes; the em dashes were left over from the earlier draft. Em dashes are also the single most commonly cited signal of machine-written text, so making them consistent removes that as well.
All 4 trailing spaces removed from the ends of paragraphs.

One phrase I left alone deliberately. Slide 5 reads “Fixed scope of work - scope creep”. It reads as a fragment rather than a point, but rewriting it would be a content decision, so it is untouched. If you want it to land, something like “Fixed scope of work — guard against scope creep” says what I think you mean.
